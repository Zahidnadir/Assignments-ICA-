2. Classmate role-play
Classmate: Why use the median if we already have the mean?

Me: The mean uses every value and can be affected by unusually high or low prices. The median is the middle value, so comparing both gives us a better picture of the data.

Classmate: Do these averages explain why prices increased?

Me: No. They summarize prices but do not prove what caused them to change. We would need more evidence to test possible causes.
